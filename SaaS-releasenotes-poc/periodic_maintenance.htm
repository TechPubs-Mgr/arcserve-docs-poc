Periodic Maintenance

Arcserve performs periodic maintenance on the Arcserve Console - SaaS to introduce new features, improve performance, and ensure service reliability.

The following impact is expected during maintenance:

  • Access to the service may be temporarily limited, which could prevent you from accessing the Arcserve Console - SaaS. This will restrict your ability to modify existing plans or create new ones.
  • Already configured or scheduled jobs will continue to function without interruption.
  • You can submit new ad-hoc backups directly using the Agent UI.
  • You can access limited restore functions through the local Agent UI.
